About us

Welcome to AppClick ICT Academy, a subsidiary of AppClick Technology – registered in Nigeria, the United States, and the United Kingdom. Our academy is dedicated to shaping tech leaders of tomorrow, delivering exceptional hands-on training in Software Engineering (frontend, backend and fullstack), Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking, AI, Data Analytics, Data Science, AI Automation, Web Design, UI/UX Design, Graphic Design, Python Programming, Java Programming, DevOps Engineering, Mobile App Development, Digital Marketing, Digital Literacy and More.

We are accredited by the American Council of Training and Development (ACTD), and Skill Development Council Canada (SDCC), and recognized as an approved trusted partner of Figma. Our certifications are globally recognized, strengthening your professional credibility and positioning you for career growth and international opportunities..

As part of the AppClick family, our academy benefits from a wealth of experiences, global conections, and a commitment to excellence. Together, we are at the forefront of empowering Tech enthusiasts and creative minds to uncover their full potentials and make lasting impacts across the board. Join us at AppClick ICT Academy, where knowledge meets opportunity, and let us embark on a journey of growth and success together. Your future in technology starts here.
